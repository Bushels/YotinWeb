## 0. Ground truth (constraints)

**Audience & voice.** WCSB cold heavy-oil production/completion/project engineers at mid-tier independents; secondary WCSB gas. Not SAGD/thermal. Technical peers; restraint reads as competence; no SaaS-speak. Kyle's bar: premium visuals (the island hero, the Blender renders); simplest robust mechanics (native scroll, no pinning gymnastics); "if something breaks, simplify rather than add".

**Creative concept — Candle in the Dark (locked).** Near-black = the unknown; one luminous element = the WellFi tool; faint cyan = transmission; candle-scale glow, soft falloff, organic — never a strobe or floodlight; formation barely resolved out of darkness, wet bitumen-saturated oilsand; relief, not triumph. **One-candle colour rule:** cyan = transmission only (collar glow, field, receiver ring, digits when a value has arrived); ember `#f27622` = surface warmth (wind, people, CTAs, eyebrows, the period in "Unknown."); sand `#e8dcc8` = readable light. Two saturated hues max per frame, never both at full strength. **EM grammar (true things only).** Insulating collar = antenna gap; low-frequency current through pipe *and* formation; the surface reads a microvolt potential between the wellhead and a remote ground stake; the field is diffusive/quasi-static, reaching up along the casing, flattened by conductive beds — never a bullet, never periodic on screen; open hole couples directly to the formation, inside casing is attenuated (qualitative labels only, source Wu et al. 2024). ## 1. Thesis

The invisible, made measurable. *yôtin* (Plains Cree: wind — a force that crosses the land without being seen) and WellFi's EM signal (crosses rock without being seen) are one idea; the site is one Clearwater well seen as a slice through rock. Scroll is a **descent** to the candle and a **return** as the signal. "Two unseen fields. One visual language." (never "one field").

## 2. Approach & architecture

- **One persistent world** (fixed canvas behind semantic DOM), one scene, one camera on authored Catmull-Rom position/target curves through chapter endpoints (desktop and mobile sets; the rig supports sub-poses), driven by a **native-scroll conductor**: `exact` progress for UI/interactions/hash/analytics; `smooth` = damped (λ 5.2) for camera/visuals. No wheel hijack, no pinning, no Lenis, no key capture. Chapters change *state* (light, cutaway, fog, focus, field) more than coordinates.
- **three.js 0.185.1, WebGLRenderer** (WebGL2 baseline; custom `onBeforeCompile` shaders — bedding, flow, field, wind). Vanilla ES modules, no framework. **Vite 8 (Rolldown)** multi-page static build (`index.html`, `privacy.html`), `public/` passthrough for allow-listed routes, robots, sitemap, assets; pinned versions + lockfile; `engines.node`; `dist/` never committed; tests run against source plus a `vite preview` smoke; CI: `npm ci && npm run build` on a clean clone. Content-hashed assets end the manual `?v=` cache-key class of bug; a regression test still checks any file that keeps a `?v=` key (e.g. `privacy.html`).
- **Motion stack:** DOM choreography = existing CSS `animation-timeline: view()` + a ~25-line IntersectionObserver fallback (fail-open, replaces GSAP entirely); hero H1 word-mask only; counters = existing `[data-count]` tween; no scramble. 3D = the conductor. Animation ownership: conductor → camera/world; CSS → outer layout wrappers; the two DOM tweens → inner spans only.
- **Post: none.** Additive halo sprites for the candle, receiver ring, wellhead. ACES tone mapping, sRGB output, exposure 1.0; candle materials `toneMapped=false`.
- **Quality tiers 0–3:** 0 = stills; 1 = phone (DPR 1.35, 320 trees, 32×20 field, no motes); 2 = tablet (1.5); 3 = desktop (1.75). Governor: ≥ 120 *settled* frames (excludes warm-up, resize, hidden), median + p95, lowers DPR first, records the reason, never oscillates. Render on demand when idle (ambient = low tick); 0 RAF when hidden or when the world is fully covered (opaque sections, ChatFi open, footer). `Timer.connect(document)`, dt clamp 1/30.
- **Import gate before any download:** `if (reduceMotion || saveData || !webgl2 || tier0) paintStills()` — modulepreload tags for the world are injected only after the gate passes.
- **Palette texture** (16×16) keys strata/steel/signal/wind so ember/sand/cyan/near-black govern the world.
- Modules: `world/{layout,wellPath,cycle,pulseMaterial,terrain,forest,wellSystem,wellfiTool,props,field,wind,readout,wellBuilder,island}.js`, `conductor.js`, `cameraRig.js`, `chapters.js` (data ledger), `interactions.js` (raycast FSM + DOM twins), `ui/{probe,rail,reveal,icons,motionToggle,stills}.js`, `boot.js`; existing `main.js` behaviours (nav, counters, qualifier render, ChatFi) modularised, not rewritten.

## 3. Geometry ledger (authoritative)

**Keep verbatim (ported in `spike/src/world/`):** slab 14 × 10 × 4.4; camera math (fov 20/30); forest instancing + PRNG 20260610 (instance colours desaturated/darkened ~⅓); lease pad (PCP drive head — never a pumpjack — tanks, separator, flare); cased J-build curve machinery; 12 s ambient light envelope (day/dusk only — the uplink *bullet* pulse is dropped); flow GLSL (sand-tinted, off in the signal chapter); bedding GLSL.

**Topology = `bench`** (production has exactly one topology; the spike's `TOPOLOGY='cavity'` branch is a dev-only comparison used to obtain Kyle's decision and is deleted before Gate 2). Solid slab; rectangular 90° notch from the front-right corner (`NOTCH = {x −1.6..7, z −0.6..5}`) cut down to the pay bench. Constants: `PAY_TOP = −2.4` (stratigraphic boundary), `BENCH_Y = PAY_TOP − 0.15 = −2.55` (notch floor and every open-hole bore centreline). Strata continuous around the shared corner; no dish, no floor mesh, no rubble. Proven in the spike (`bench-lit.png`, 47 calls / 63 k tris).

**Section-cut convention (render semantics):** the cased J-build is a *positive* steel tubular on the outer front face (z = +5, x < −1.6); the open-hole trunk and legs are *negative* dark rock-walled troughs whose centreline lies on the bench plane, buried slightly past half so a rock lip overhangs (no metallic material on open-hole legs); a hairline sand cut-edge on every cut boundary; bedding continuous across bench and both notch walls; baked contact darkening in the inner corner and along the wall/bench junction (a flat evenly lit bench reads as a stage floor). **At least two legs leave the notch**: their toes pass z = −0.6 into solid rock and terminate as bore mouths (dark ellipse, thin sand rim) on the back cut face; the ch. 4 x-ray reveals their continuation as ghosted hairlines with the pay band lit faintly around them ("two legs continue beyond the section"). The cased curve already crosses the x = −1.6 wall at ≈ (−1.6, −2.5, 3.9) — name that bore mouth too. One-time caption at first exposure: "Section cut exposes the open-hole bores." A small cross-section witness ring at one exposed toe (700 ms) the first time a lateral legend item receives focus.

**Strata (Clearwater):** topsoil 0→−0.35 `#3f5a2e` · Colorado Shale (seal) −0.35→−1.5 `#6f787e` fine lamination (freq 38) · Clearwater Upper Sand −1.5→−2.1 `#a8874f` · Middle Mudstone −2.1→−2.4 `#4a4540` tight beds · Clearwater Lower Sand (pay) −2.4→−3.6 `#2b1e14` roughness 0.45 (bench at −2.55) · McMurray (Lower Mannville) −3.6→−4.4 `#241a10` (label corrected round 11; band name in code stays `ellerslie` as an internal id). Labels = names only, DOM-projected sand hairlines, custody in `chapters.js`.

**Well:** casing telescope 0.175 (surface collar, top 15–20 % of the vertical) > 0.127 (7-in shell) > 0.100 (cased) > 0.086 (open-hole trunk) > 0.070 (legs); the J-build rides half-proud on the front cut face; heel on the bench; trunk flat across the bench (gentle non-straightness); **8 legs from 8 staggered stations, alternating sides of the trunk** (round 14a, Kyle: *"We need fish scale drilling on the other side as well of the OHML"*) — t = 0.12/0.22/0.34/0.44/0.56/0.65/0.77/0.87, sides +/−/+/−/+/−/+/−, never two legs at one station and never a mirrored pair; each leg leaves at 42–48° off the local trunk tangent, flat in the pay band at `BENCH_Y`; the open-side (+) legs are short because the notch floor runs out at z = +5, the back-side (−) legs are longer and the two longest cross the z = −0.6 wall into solid rock (bore mouths, above). Authored as `LEGS = {t, side, deg, len}` in `wellPath.js` and **derived** from the trunk, so the toes cannot drift off it. Leg bores merge into one mesh and run coarser tessellation than the trunk, so doubling the leg count costs no draw calls and fewer triangles; shoe torus at the heel. Tool placements: `outside-intermediate` (open-hole trunk u ≈ 0.03, at the heel on the bench, ~1 unit from the x = −1.6 wall) and `below-pump` (cased u = 0.5, inside the 7-in string).

**The candle lives at the open-hole anchor.** Chapters 0–5 show the collar at `outside-intermediate` (heel, on the bench): the chapter-3 thesis "the formation is the antenna" is then literally true in the frame — collar in open hole, field through rock on the bench and the x = −1.6 wall, return current up the casing on the outer front face, reading at the wellhead, stake on the road. Chapter 6's qualifier is what walks the collar back inside casing (`below-pump`) when the visitor answers "shallower"; the field visibly weakens and the coupling label flips to *inside casing — attenuated*.

**Tool:** parametric, four named volumes (sleeve, isolation gap, sensor package, battery), less detail than the approved ghost render, ~2 k tris, orbit clamped ±0.3 rad, chip "schematic — see the tool figure below". No GLB.

**Colour & light:** cyan candle at the collar (red retired); flow chevrons sand-tinted at the surface, off in ch. 3; digits/ring sand until ch. 3; ch. 0 golden hour (sun lower/warmer), dusk from ch. 1, darkest at ch. 3, relight from ch. 4.

**Field (`field.js`):** a section-plane field on exactly four planes — the bench (y = −2.55), the x = −1.6 notch wall, the z = −0.6 notch wall, and the outer front face (z = +5, x < −1.6, carrying the return up the casing) — plus one additive halo at the collar. **It must read as a geophysical section, not an LED wall:** instances are short strokes **oriented along the local field gradient**, placed on a **blue-noise jittered** lattice (not Cartesian), **culled below a magnitude threshold** (never alpha-faded to a dim grid), density falling with magnitude, screen coverage capped (additive fill is the laptop risk — cull, don't fade). Potential: anisotropic quasi-static (dipole-ish falloff, vertical reach along the casing, flattened/dimmed above the Colorado seal); pointer/finger probe memory via a 128×1 RGBA DataTexture (1.5 s decay) that responds to click/tap or a 200 ms dwell — never to a pointer merely travelling. **Not periodic:** one swell on chapter entry, one on activation, idle = low-amplitude **noise-driven, non-propagating** modulation (no `sin(t + r)` terms); persistent label "visual timing not representative". Candle scale: thin collar band brightest, ≤ 40 % alpha at peak, exponential falloff. Tier 1 = 32×20 strokes, one draw call.

## 5. Interaction contract

- **Cursor = probe** (canvas zones only, ≥ 1100 px + fine pointer, suppressed while scrolling): additive sand ring, transform-only, native cursor visible; reveals rock, x-rays the tool, locks on hotspots; the only word chip is "Inspect" on the orbit affordance; self-disables on blur/hidden/frames > 40 ms.
- **Buttons:** ember primary, sand secondary; hover = ember sweep; press = 1 px inset; focus ring always; no idle animation; loading/disabled/error styled.
- **Icons:** inline SVG sprite; 8 animations (gauge sweep, thermometer fill, waveform, drop fill, trend draw, wifi arcs, plug connect, plus rotate) triggered once by hover/focus/enter, none idle; feather glyph replaced by a neutral mark.
- **Text:** hero H1 word-mask only; counters settle; digits appear first when the circuit closes.
- **Scroll:** native; rail = casing tally + chapter nav (real anchors, `aria-current`, exists from first paint), descent/return twin indicators (sand marker descends, cyan indicator returns); hash sync via `replaceState`; anchor arrival = hard cut (set `exact`, snap pose, load group with placeholder, 240 ms fade); header keeps "Check your well fit" after `[data-scrolled]`; hero → Q1 in ≤ 1 interaction / ≤ 1.5 s (Playwright).
- **Touch:** one tap for cards/FAQ/nav/CTAs; two-stage only for spatial controls (stake, orbit); `touch-action: pan-y` always; readouts/tally in a collapsed 36 px bar (never open by default); no orientation.
- **Keyboard/AT:** DOM order = story order; every raycast target has a **visible** twin ≥ 44 px (CI shape test); roving tabindex only inside a focused group; canvas `aria-hidden`; sr readout static + "representative"; live region once per chapter/user action; `Esc` exits orbit.
- **Motion control:** fixed-layer pause toggle from first paint of the world (`aria-pressed`, persisted, stops all ambient incl. DOM idles; disclosed in privacy notice).
- **Loading:** HTML + CTAs + new ch. 0 poster first (LCP); import gate; modulepreload after the gate; two rAFs; `compileAsync`; 800 ms `.is-live` fade; 4 s fail-safe; primitives on screen immediately; chapter groups prefetched by exact progress + direction.
- **ChatFi:** panel/adapter/ARIA unchanged; context = fixed states (`chapter`, `qualifier_pending|fit_strong|fit_review|fit_future`).

## 13b. Self-improvement loop (rounds 1–6, 2026-08-19)

Three critics (Opus 5 · Codex GPT-5.6-Sol · a craft critic) review the captured frame set each round; every finding
is adversarially verified against the frames **and the source** before it is acted on, and refuted findings are
recorded rather than fixed. Scores rose 4.0 / 4.5 / 5.5 → 6.4 / 6.0 / 8.2 across five rounds. What the loop
changed that the spec did not anticipate, and which is now binding:

- **The camera map must be continuous.** A one-pose step at a chapter boundary is invisible in a still and
  unmissable in motion, and it made the frame job non-deterministic (the same anchor rendered two different poses
  1e-4 apart in progress). `test/pose-continuity.test.js` asserts every boundary. The chapter-5 anchor is the one
  deliberate exception: it is mid-rise.
- **A control must be spatially true, not just captioned.** "No difference, no reading" now means the stake stands
  on the wellhead and no loop is drawn; the loop opens instantly and only closing is eased.
- **Legibility beats fidelity for a hairline.** A 1 px GL line, a 0.004 torus and a 0.02 stake were all correct and
  all invisible; they are tubes, rules and a taller stake now.
- **Frame-rate-dependent easing is a bug, not a detail.** A per-frame 0.06 step is ~1 s at 60 fps and ~20 s on a
  software renderer or a slow phone — the verdict's candle appeared never to light. All state easing is time-based.
- **Copy grounds are washes, never panes.** A flat scrim rectangle puts a one-pixel luminance step across the
  render; grounds feather over ~140 px and their bleed is clipped so it cannot widen the document.
- **Gate on the chapter index, not raw progress.** `floor(p + 0.002)` is the only correct chapter test; comparing
  raw `exact` against integers left cyan on the rail at 3.9998.
- **Two new CI gates** came out of the loop: `scripts/colour-gate.mjs` (saturated cyan only in the chapter-3 still,
  and no still older than `src/chapters.js`) and the runtime/overflow/one-candle assertions in `scripts/frames.mjs
  --strict`. Both caught real regressions the tests could not.
